Government Schemes · NSW

First home buyer benefits in New South Wales

$10,000
First Home Owner (New Homes) Grant

NSW has two main schemes. The First Home Owner (New Homes) Grant provides $10,000 towards a newly built home (no-one has lived in it before). The First Home Buyers Assistance Scheme provides a full stamp duty exemption on new or existing homes up to $800,000, and a concession on homes up to $1,000,000 –saving up to $30,000. Federally, the First Home Guarantee lets eligible buyers purchase with just 5% deposit and no LMI.

Common Questions

Home loans in Sydney – answered

Can I buy in Sydney with a 5% deposit?
Yes. Through the federal First Home Guarantee scheme, eligible first home buyers can purchase with just a 5% deposit and avoid paying Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) — saving $20,000–$40,000 in upfront costs. Income and property price caps apply. We assess your eligibility as part of your initial strategy call.
How much can I borrow on a $120,000 salary in Sydney?
On a $120,000 gross salary with typical living expenses, most lenders will assess borrowing capacity in the range of $600,000–$720,000 — though this varies significantly by lender, your expenses, existing debts and credit history. Some specialist lenders assess income differently and may offer more. We run your numbers across multiple lenders to find your real ceiling, not just one bank's answer.
What Sydney suburbs qualify for first home buyer stamp duty exemptions?
The NSW stamp duty exemption applies to the purchase price, not the suburb — new homes up to $800,000 are fully exempt, with concessions up to $1,000,000. This opens up options in many western and outer-ring suburbs including Penrith, Liverpool, Campbelltown, Blacktown and the Hills District. We map out which areas fit your budget and grant eligibility.
How long does formal mortgage approval take in NSW?
Once we submit a complete application, most lenders return formal approval within 3–10 business days for standard purchases. Complex scenarios — self-employed income, unusual property types, tight timeframes — can take longer. We prepare your application thoroughly upfront to avoid back-and-forth, and we know which lenders move fastest when time matters.
Can I use equity from my Sydney property to buy an investment?
Yes — and it's one of the most common strategies we help clients with. If your Sydney property has grown in value, you may be able to access that equity as a deposit for your next purchase without saving from scratch. We calculate your usable equity, identify the right loan structure and help you buy your next property without selling the first.
